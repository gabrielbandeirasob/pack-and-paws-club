Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026 · Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pack & Paws Club mobile application (the “App”) collects, uses, shares and protects information. The App is operated by AutoNest Development (“we”, “us”) for the dog daycare, boarding and transport business that uses it (the “Business”).

1. Information we collect

Account and team information

  • Name, email address, phone number and role (manager or driver) of the people who use the App.
  • Organization name and business contact details.

Client, pet and booking information

  • Pet owner details you enter: name, phone number, email address, home address and access instructions (for example gate or door codes needed for pickup).
  • Pet details: name, breed, notes, vaccination and care information you choose to record.
  • Bookings: daycare and boarding dates, transport needs and route assignments.

Contacts (optional)

  • If you choose to import a contact to create a client, the App reads the contact you select. The App does not upload your whole address book.

Location (drivers only)

  • While a driver has a published route for the day and the App is open, the driver’s device location is shared with the Business’s dispatch screen so managers can see progress and estimated arrival times.
  • Location sharing stops when the route is finished, when no route is published, or when the driver closes the App. Location is never collected in the background.
  • Dispatch location samples are retained for a maximum of 24 hours and then deleted automatically.

Diagnostic information

  • Basic technical information needed to keep the App working (for example error messages and app version).

2. How we use information

  • Provide the service: manage clients, pets and bookings; assign and publish routes; let drivers see their stops, addresses and access instructions.
  • Coordinate transport: show managers route progress, estimated arrival times and any problem reported by a driver.
  • Communicate with you about your account, invitations and service messages.
  • Keep the App secure and reliable, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How information is shared

  • Within the Business: managers see client, pet, booking and route information; drivers only see the routes assigned to them.
  • Service providers: we use trusted providers to run the App, including Supabase (database, authentication and hosting) and Apple (App Store distribution). If map services are enabled, routing and address data may be processed by the map provider to calculate routes and travel times.
  • Legal: we may disclose information when required by law or to protect the safety of people and animals.

4. Data retention

  • Driver location samples: automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Route data (stops, order, status and times): kept while the Business uses the App, so history and reports work.
  • Client, pet and booking records: kept while the Business account is active, or until a deletion request is made.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with row-level access controls, so each Business only sees its own records and each driver only sees their own route.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Drivers can decline or revoke location permission in iOS Settings at any time; route actions still work, but dispatch visibility and arrival estimates will be unavailable.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing automadigitalsup@gmail.com.
  • Pet owners should contact the Business that provides their pet care service, or email us and we will forward the request to the Business.

7. Children

The App is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The effective date at the top will change and material updates will be communicated in the App or by email.

Summary: we collect what the Business needs to run daycare, boarding and transport — client and pet details, bookings, team accounts and, only during an active route, the driver’s location, which is deleted after 24 hours.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: automadigitalsup@gmail.com.
