Common questions

How do I add a client and their dog?

Open Clients → Add client. You can import a contact or type the details. Address and access instructions are used by drivers during pickup.

How do drivers get today’s route?

Managers assign stops in Dispatch and tap Publish. The route appears in the driver’s app immediately, with each stop’s address and access instructions.

What happens if the driver loses internet?

The driver app keeps working with the saved route. Actions marked offline are stored on the device and sync automatically when the connection returns.

Why does the app ask for location?

Drivers may share their location during an active route so the manager can follow progress and estimated arrival times. Sharing is optional and stops when the route ends. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email automadigitalsup@gmail.com from your account address and we will process the request.

Report a problem

Include your account email, the screen you were on and, if possible, a screenshot. Bugs that block a route should be marked Problem in the driver app so dispatch is notified immediately.
